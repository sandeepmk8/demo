Seventeen years of charts, and still learning

My grandmother kept a school notebook with the birth time of everyone in the family, written down the day each of us arrived. That notebook is the reason I do this work. I began formal study in 2001 under Sri Venkatesha Bhatta in Melkote, eight years before I felt ready to charge anyone for a reading.

I practise Parashari jyotisha, with Jaimini techniques where a question calls for them. Divisional charts get used when they earn their place and not as a flourish. I don't do vague reassurance and I don't do doom — a chart shows conditions, not verdicts, and most of a good sitting is spent working out what can actually be done with the years in front of you.

Around four hundred readings a year now, roughly half of them over video, for people who left Bengaluru and still want someone who reads the way their family did.

The nine grahas

Nine bodies, each governing a stretch of ordinary life. A chart is largely the story of where they sat on the day you were born, and which one is running things now.

Surya

the Sun

Father, authority, vitality, and the self you cannot keep hidden for long.

Chandra

the Moon

Mother, mind, mood and memory. The fastest of them, and the one that colours all the rest.

Mangala

Mars

Siblings, land, appetite, surgery, and the temper you keep on a short rope.

Budha

Mercury

Speech, trade, calculation, paperwork, and the friends you can actually talk to.

Guru

Jupiter

Teachers, children, belief, and the kind of luck that follows from being decent.

Shukra

Venus

Marriage, comfort, art, vehicles, and whatever it is you find beautiful.

Shani

Saturn

Work, delay, old age, and everything that is only ever earned the slow way.

Rahu

the north node

Hunger with no ceiling. Foreign things, obsession, sudden rise, and the fog around it.

Ketu

the south node

Detachment, loss, sharp insight, and the things you are quietly finished with.

Why timing is most of the work

A chart is fixed; what changes is which graha is running. The Vimshottari cycle divides a hundred and twenty years between the nine, in a set order and in these lengths. Where your birth falls in that sequence decides which stretch of life you are in now. Tap a period to see what it tends to bring.

One hundred and twenty years The full cycle, in the order Ketu, Shukra, Surya, Chandra, Mangala, Rahu, Guru, Shani, Budha. Most people live through five or six of them.

How a reading works

Four steps, and about a week from first message to written note.

You send your birth details

Date, time and place of birth, for everyone the reading concerns. A hospital record or a family note is ideal. Guesses are workable, but say plainly that they are guesses.

I cast and study the chart beforehand

Two or three hours of work that happens before we speak, so the sitting itself goes on your questions rather than on arithmetic. If something in the chart is unclear, I will write and ask before we meet.

We sit down, on Bull Temple Road or on a call

Bring your questions written down. The sittings that go well are the ones where you decided in advance what you actually want to know. Family may join; nobody has to.

You get the recording and a written note

Sent the same evening. Almost nobody remembers a reading accurately a month later, and the recording is the part clients tell me they use most.

What a reading can and can't do

Worth reading before you book, so that nobody's time gets wasted.

What it can do

  • Show which years carry weight and which are quiet
  • Name the pattern you keep repeating and can't see from inside it
  • Give you a defensible reason to wait, or to move now
  • Put a difficult stretch in proportion, including how long it runs
  • Settle an argument at home about a date

What it can't do

  • Tell you the date of a death, or read a terminal illness
  • Stand in for a doctor, a lawyer or a therapist
  • Guarantee an outcome, however clear the chart looks
  • Make the decision on your behalf
  • Undo something with a stone or a thread

Questions

The ones that come up before almost every first sitting.

I don't know my exact birth time

Common, and workable. There is a process called birth-time rectification, where we work backwards from events you can date precisely — a marriage, a move, a surgery, a job that started on a known day — to narrow the ascendant. It needs one extra sitting and costs ₹2,000 on top. Without it some parts of the chart stay unreadable, and I will tell you which ones.

Where do I find my birth time?

In order of reliability: the hospital discharge summary, the municipal birth certificate (Indian hospital births usually record the time), a horoscope written by a family astrologer at the time, and finally family memory. Memory is far more accurate when it is anchored to something else — the milk delivery, the morning news, someone leaving for the office.

Do you read charts for children?

Only for parents asking about health or schooling, and I keep those short. I don't read a child's marriage or career prospects. It puts a weight on them that isn't fair, and it changes how they get treated at home.

Will you tell me something frightening?

I won't predict a death or read a terminal illness, and I don't answer questions framed that way. Where a difficult period shows in the chart I will say so plainly, tell you roughly how long it runs, and then spend the rest of the time on what can be done about it.

Do you sell gemstones or arrange pujas?

No. If a remedy involves buying something I will name what is needed and you can source it wherever you like. I take no commission from any jeweller, priest or temple, and I never will.

How do I pay?

UPI or bank transfer, after the sitting rather than before. Details come with the confirmation message. There is a sliding fee for students and for anyone between jobs — just say so when you write.

Can I bring my parents?

Yes, and for compatibility readings it usually helps. For a personal reading I'd rather we speak alone for the first half hour and bring them in after.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Charts and notes stay on one offline machine. I don't discuss a client's chart with anyone, including family members who booked the sitting on their behalf.
